Our Story

From construction to development.

The ALDA story is not a story that began with a single project. It is an evolution of a family business journey spanning generations, combined with professional experience in the real estate industry and hands-on construction expertise. The business has grown from its earlier identity as Sri Shreenivas Enterprises into a more structured and contemporary brand under ALDA.

As a contractor, we understand what it takes to plan and execute a project on the ground. As a developer, we understand location, design, customer requirements, quality, timelines and long-term value.
